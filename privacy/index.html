Molymorph ("we", "us", or "our") explains below how information is handled in the Android app "くるりんイライラ棒" (English title: Buzz Wire: Spin Challenge; the "App") and on the website hosting this Policy. This Policy also applies to other language versions of the same App.

1. Game Records and On-Device Storage

The App stores information on your device, including progress, unlock status, keys earned, completion times, records of LIFE added through ads, display language, audio settings, and records relating to guidance and review requests. The App has no user account system of its own and no feature that sends progress to our servers for synchronization.

Depending on your Android settings, operating system, and device, some saved data may be included in Android's standard backup or device-to-device transfer. We have no feature that allows us to access those backups. For deletion and restoration, see "6. Deleting Saved Records and Backups".

2. Information Handled in Connection with Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob's ad-serving software (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display rewarded ads for watching videos and ads when switching screens. Depending on the SDK, device settings, region, your choices, and other factors, Google and businesses involved in ad delivery may collect, transmit, and use the following information.

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from the IP address
  • Advertising ID, App Set ID, temporary identifiers generated within the ad-serving software, and similar identifiers
  • Usage information, such as app launches, ad impressions, taps, and video views
  • Technical information about failures, diagnostics, and performance (such as app, SDK, and OS versions, device model, connectivity status, and error information)

This information is used for purposes such as ad delivery and personalization within the scope permitted for each identifier, measuring and analyzing ad effectiveness, maintaining services, and detecting and preventing fraud. App Set ID is not used for ad personalization or ad effectiveness measurement, and is not linked to identifiers such as the Advertising ID or to personal information for advertising purposes.

Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted (TLS). Please also consult each provider's explanations about how external services handle information and how long they retain it.

Ad personalization depends on Google and device settings, your choices, and applicable requirements. Even non-personalized ads may involve the handling of information for purposes such as frequency management, aggregation, and fraud prevention.

3. Ad Privacy Settings

If ad privacy settings appear in the App's settings screen, you can change your choices there. When consent needs to be requested, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID through Android's ad settings, where these options are available. This action is separate from deleting information that has already been processed. Please consult Google's explanations about its use of data and the controls available to you.

6. Deleting Saved Records and Backups

Saved game records remain on your device until you reset or delete the data or take similar action. You can reset on-device records through Android's "Settings → Apps → the App → Storage" by selecting "Clear storage" or "Clear data". Labels and steps vary by device. Clearing the cache alone does not delete the records.

Clearing on-device data is separate from deleting cloud backups. Records may be restored from an Android backup, for example when you reinstall the App after uninstalling it. Manage backups through Android or your Google Account settings.

Retention periods for advertising-related information independently handled by Google or other providers, and for Android backups, are governed by the respective services' terms and policies and your settings.
